Frequently Asked Questions

How do I delete my account?

Send an email to support@usestepzero.com with the subject "Delete my account". We'll remove all your data within 30 days.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your subscription at any time through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android) subscription settings.

I forgot my password — what do I do?

Use the "Forgot password" option on the login screen. You'll receive an email with a reset link.

My streak disappeared — can it be restored?

Contact us at support@usestepzero.com and we'll look into it for you.
